# Official Motto and Organizational Architecture

**Official organization motto:**  
**Committed to Glorify, Passion to Evangelize**

**Status:** Proposed for adoption through this pull request. Once merged, this document is the canonical source for the motto and its organizational interpretation.

## Meaning

- **Committed to Glorify** — pursue work with integrity, humility, excellence, stewardship, and a purpose larger than individual recognition.
- **Passion to Evangelize** — communicate the organization's values, mission, and useful work with clarity, respect, and genuine enthusiasm. In contexts where the word *evangelize* has a religious meaning, preserve that meaning without coercion and respect the audience's beliefs and choice.

The motto is an expression of organizational purpose. It is not a substitute for customer consent, privacy, security, legal obligations, evidence, or human authorization.

## How the motto applies across GLORIFIER

1. **Leadership and authority:** the human owner and designated leaders are accountable for translating the motto into clear priorities, responsible decisions, and reviewable outcomes.
2. **Product and architecture:** build useful, interoperable, accessible, and maintainable systems; document limitations honestly; do not claim a capability is live or verified without evidence.
3. **Governance and safety:** identity, least privilege, policy, approval, auditability, and fail-closed behavior remain mandatory. The motto never authorizes bypassing safeguards.
4. **Customer relationships:** explain services and pricing clearly, obtain consent for communications, respect boundaries, and avoid misleading claims or pressure.
5. **Engineering and operations:** prefer reliable work, transparent status, accountable ownership, measured improvements, and safe reversibility over appearances or unsupported promises.
6. **Communication and community:** share the mission in language appropriate to the audience, with respect for differing beliefs and without implying endorsement or affiliation that does not exist.
7. **Evidence and stewardship:** measure outcomes honestly, protect personal data and secrets, correct mistakes openly, and use resources responsibly.

## Architectural placement

The motto belongs to the organization's **Purpose and Values** layer. It informs leadership priorities, product principles, communication, and review criteria, while existing authority and control layers govern execution:

```text
Official Motto and Organizational Purpose
                 |
Leadership, Values, and Responsible Stewardship
                 |
Product Principles and Enterprise Architecture
                 |
GEAS: Identity, Policy, Risk, Authority, Approval
                 |
Governed Services, Agents, Integrations, and Operations
                 |
Evidence, Reconciliation, and Outcome Review
```

The motto does not grant an AI agent, service, or employee additional permissions. Existing authorization, privacy, security, financial controls, and human approval requirements remain in force.

## Adoption and consistency

- Use the exact official wording: **Committed to Glorify, Passion to Evangelize**.
- Use this document as the canonical reference when reproducing or interpreting the motto.
- Keep the motto distinct from the technical product positioning and the GLR economic-unit tagline; they serve different purposes.
- Update public-facing brand materials and organization-owned architecture documents in reviewed changes rather than assuming that this repository change automatically updates external profiles, websites, or accounts.
- Review future changes for consistency with the motto without weakening evidence requirements, safety controls, or respect for customer choice.
